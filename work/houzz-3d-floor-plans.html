About houzz

Houzz brings homeowners, inspiration, products, and professionals together around home renovation and design. Houzz Pro supports the professional side of that journey, helping contractors and designers manage projects, clients, and their day-to-day work.

My work at houzz

During my time at Houzz, I worked across a wide range of Houzz Pro areas, including project management, timelines, product libraries, budgets, and reporting. I also contributed to the design system and rebrand, which gave me a broader view of how the product fit together.

3D Floor Plans became the area I loved most. It brought together complex technology, the physical world, and tools that let users be creative too. Pros could shape spaces, explore ideas, and help clients see what they imagined.
