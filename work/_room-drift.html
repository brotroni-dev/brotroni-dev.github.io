Rooms that never connect

A candidate hero for Multi-Room Scan. Each room is drawn the way the product draws one - heavy walls, a pale floor, dimensions on two sides, and the ghost of the previous shape behind it. They turn, drift, and bump into each other, and never once join up.

Preview only - nothing on the live case study has changed. Count, speed, spin and the size range are all easy to tune.
