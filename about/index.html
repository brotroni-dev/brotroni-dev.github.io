About me

I'm a senior product designer based in Tel Aviv, working across web and mobile.

How I got here

After graduating from Shenkar, I managed an artist's gallery, worked in social media, and later worked as a content editor.

The projects I enjoyed most weren't purely visual. They also involved understanding people and figuring out how something should work. That led me to study UX/UI and join a design agency, where I worked with clients in healthcare, banking, drone technology, and corporate training.

Working across short-term projects made me want to stay involved after launch and see how products evolved over time. I joined an in-house product team and have since worked across a wide range of product areas on web and mobile.

Outside of work

When I'm not working, I'm usually volunteering at an animal sanctuary, drawing, or making natural products as a certified aromatherapist.
